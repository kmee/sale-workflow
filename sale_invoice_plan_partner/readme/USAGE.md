- Create a quotation, tick *Use Invoice Plan* and choose the *Payers*
  mode in the *Invoice Plan* tab.
- Add one line per payer with its percentage. The payers must add up to
  100% when the order is confirmed. Payers can still be changed after
  the confirmation, until the order is invoiced.
- Optionally set a *Fixed Payer* on order lines that go whole to one of
  the payers.
- On the confirmed order, *Create Invoice by Plan* creates one invoice
  per payer.
- The payer of an invoiced installment cannot be changed: cancel its
  invoices first.
