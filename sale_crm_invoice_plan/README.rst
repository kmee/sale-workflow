Lets the salesperson note on the opportunity who pays the deal and the
share of each payer, before the quotation exists.

The *Invoicing* tab of the opportunity is filled from the invoice plan
template of the customer (``sale_invoice_plan_template``) and can be
edited. New quotations of the opportunity start with these payers in
their invoice plan by payer (``sale_invoice_plan_partner``), in place of
the template of the customer.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Usage
=====

- On an opportunity, open the *Invoicing* tab and list the payers with
  their percentages. Choosing the customer loads its template, if any.
- Click *New Quotation*: the quotation starts with the *Payers* invoice
  plan filled in from the opportunity.
